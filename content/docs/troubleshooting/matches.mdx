## Match cancelled

**Why:** Host left before start, not enough ready players, or the lobby expired.

**Fix:** For paid lobbies, check Wallet activity for a refund. Then start or join a new lobby.

**Retry:** After any refund shows (paid), or immediately (free).

**Support:** Entry taken with no refund after cancellation — include tx ids.

## Actions rejected / game stuck

**Why:** Moved out of turn, outdated screen, or the match already ended.

**Fix:** Wait a moment for the board to update; refresh the room once.

**Retry:** After the board looks current. If the match ended, use the result / claim UI.

## Rewards missing after a win

**Why:** Claim not finished, wrong account, or settle still processing.

**Fix:** Follow [Playing → Rewards](/user/playing) and [Wallet claims](/troubleshooting/wallet-transactions).

**Retry:** From Wallet → pending wins after the result screen.

**Support:** Include lobby id, game id, and any claim tx ids.
