## Contact support

Escalate when:

- A vault tx confirmed on Hiro but the app never credits join/claim after several minutes and retries.
- You were charged / funds moved incorrectly with tx ids as proof.
- Account access is broken after clearing cookies and trying a second browser.

Include: account email or username, lobby id / path, game id, Stacks tx ids, UTC timestamps, and screenshots of errors.
