---
title: Wallet and transactions
description: Insufficient balance, pending txs, and claim delays.
---

## Insufficient balance

**Why:** Cached or true on-chain balance is below entry / withdraw amount.

**Fix:** Deposit USDCx to your custodial address; wait for confirmation; refresh Wallet.

**Retry:** After Hiro shows the deposit; allow a short cache delay (about a few minutes max).

## Pending blockchain transaction

**Why:** Stacks tx is in mempool or awaiting confirmations; UI is waiting on Hiro.

**Fix:** Keep the tab open or note the tx id on a explorer. Do not start a conflicting second join/withdraw for the same intent.

**Retry:** After the tx succeeds or fails definitively. Never assume a dropped tx succeeded.

## Transaction confirmation delays

**Why:** Network congestion or indexer lag.

**Fix:** Wait; verify tx status on Hiro / explorer. Refresh balance/claims afterward.

**Retry:** Soft retry of **status check** anytime; avoid rebroadcasting unless the app explicitly offers it after failure.

## Claim not showing / stuck

**Why:** Match settle not finished, claim intent not yet issued, or vault claim tx not submitted.

**Fix:** Open Wallet → pending wins; run claim again. Confirm you are the recipient.

**Retry:** After settle UI appears; space retries by a minute if the chain is slow.

**Support:** Confirmed winning match, settle UI shown, claim tx confirmed, balance never moved.
