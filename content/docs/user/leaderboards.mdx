## Seasons

Competitive play accumulates **season points** from settled matches. The leaderboard shows the current season’s ranking.

- Rank and points on a profile come from the same season source as the leaderboard.
- Favourite games and season history appear on public profiles.

## Updating standings

Points apply after a match is fully settled (engine complete + platform persistence). Mid-match disconnects follow each game’s forfeit rules before points are finalized.

If your rank looks stale, hard-refresh the leaderboard after settle completes.
