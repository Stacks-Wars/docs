## Overview

Your play balance is **USDCx** on your Stacks address. The Wallet page shows:

- Available balance
- Deposit address
- Withdraw form
- Pending win claims
- Recent activity (deposits, withdraws, lobby stakes, claims)

## Funding

1. Open **Wallet** → copy your deposit address.
2. Send **USDCx** on Stacks (mainnet).
3. Wait for confirmation, then refresh if the balance has not updated yet.

Deposit enough to cover the entry fee before creating or joining paid rooms.

## Withdrawals

1. Enter an amount within the allowed min/max.
2. Follow the withdraw steps in the UI.
3. Wait for the Stacks transaction to confirm before retrying.

See [Transaction issues](/troubleshooting/wallet-transactions) if something stays pending.
