## Create an account

1. Open [stackswars.com](https://stackswars.com) and choose **Sign in**.
2. Sign up with email or Google.
3. For email signup, enter the **6-digit verification code** sent to your inbox (disposable / burner addresses are rejected).
4. Set a **username** (and optionally display name / avatar) under **Settings**.

## Sign in

Use the same sign-in flow. If it fails:

- Clear site cookies for the app and retry.
- Confirm you are on [stackswars.com](https://stackswars.com).
- See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).

## After you sign in

- A **Stacks wallet** is created for you automatically — you deposit **USDCx** there to play.
- Balance and lobbies update in real time while you are connected.

You do not need a browser wallet extension.

## Developer ID

If you build games, open **your own profile** and click **Get ID**. Copy that UUID into your game’s `dev_id`. The control is hidden when viewing other players.
