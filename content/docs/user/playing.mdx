## Starting a game

1. Fill the lobby to the game’s minimum players.
2. Ready up when the UI asks.
3. The host starts the match.

If start is blocked, check that everyone is ready and still connected.

## During the match

Play in the room UI. If your connection drops, wait for reconnect or refresh the room — you usually return to the same match.

Quitting mid-match follows that game’s forfeit rules.

## Match completion

When the match ends you see a **result** screen. Season points and stats update after it settles.

## Rewards and claims

For **paid** matches:

1. Winners claim payouts from the result screen (or later from **Wallet**).
2. Pending claims stay under Wallet until you finish them.

Free matches award stats and points only — no cash claim.

Unclaimed rewards stay available; the lobby link may disappear after the room closes.
