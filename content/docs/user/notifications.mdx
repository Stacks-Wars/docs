## Notifications

Short **toasts** confirm actions and surface errors. Lobby and room updates arrive in real time while you are connected.

Community updates also live on [Telegram](https://t.me/stackswars).

## What you can do

- Sign in and manage your profile
- Deposit / withdraw USDCx
- Browse games and open lobbies (free or paid)
- Private lobbies with join requests
- Play live matches and claim winnings
- Seasons, leaderboard, and public profiles

First-party games: Checkers, Lexi Wars, Ludo, Ludo Rush.
